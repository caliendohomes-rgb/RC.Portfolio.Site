01 / Portfolio & platform In progress

A shared foundation.
A coordinated migration.

Turning a multi-product Backup portfolio strategy into sequenced execution toward a natively built object-storage data plane.

My role
Program leadership across Product, Engineering, and cross-functional dependencies.
The execution system
Migration workstreams. Jira-aligned roadmaps. Source-linked dashboards. Clear escalation paths.

Cyber Resilience / Backup SaaS / Cloud readiness

Inside the program

Where the ambiguity lived

Teams did not yet share migration definitions, ownership, sequencing, or roadmap maturity. Some Product and Engineering leads were unclear how their work mapped to the broader migration.

What I put in place

Migration workstreams, epic hygiene standards for Product Owners, a portfolio dashboard tied to source epics, per-product migration sessions, office hours, and escalation paths for unresolved decisions.

The decisions around delivery

Sequencing depends on Architecture, Infrastructure, Security, Finance, cloud-provider strategy, customer migration, and regulatory requirements. I distinguish executable commitments from milestones contingent on upstream decisions.

The strategic context

The shared foundation supports objectives for lower infrastructure cost, fewer strategic platforms, public-cloud deployment, and FedRAMP and regulated-market readiness. These are program objectives, not claims of achieved savings or certification.
